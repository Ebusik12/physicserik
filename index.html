Let me introduce myself

I am an experimental physicist specializing in levitational optomechanics at the Department of Theoretical Physics and Astrophysics, Institute of Scientific Instruments of the CAS. I focus on making complex experimental systems measurable, repeatable, and useful.

I enjoy clear communication of experiments and mentoring students in hands-on research.

For a detailed overview of my education, research experience, skills, and talks, please see my dedicated CV page.

Every so often, I create a small piece of digital art or write down my thoughts. Sometimes, it will be possible to find it in the Blog section.
