About this site

This site is a small hub for my work and interests as an experimental physicist in levitational optomechanics. It is meant to be lightweight, easy to maintain, and focused on clear writing rather than flashy design.

You’ll mainly find:

  • A short overview of who I am on the front page
  • A more structured summary of my background on the CV page
  • Occasional posts and notes in the Posts section

What I like working on

I enjoy problems where physics, experiments, and precise measurement meet. In practice that means:

  • Designing and characterizing levitational optomechanical systems
  • Writing reusable tools to automate experiment control and analysis
  • Explaining experimental results in a way that colleagues and students can build on

About the technology

This site is built with Jekyll and the Hydejack theme, which generate static HTML pages. That makes it fast, portable, and easy to host on services like GitHub Pages or Netlify.

If you are curious about how everything is put together, the full source code of this site lives in the same repository that powers the page you are reading now.
